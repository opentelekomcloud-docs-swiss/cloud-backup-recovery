Changing the Retention Rule for Existing Backups
================================================

(Experimental Feature)

.. note::

   This feature is currently available for internal testing purposes only. It is not intended for customer use. Please do not use it in production environments.

#. Log in to the CBR console.

   a. In the upper left corner, click |image1| and select a region.
   b. Click |image2| and choose **Storage** > **Cloud Backup and Recovery**.

#. On any backup page, locate the target vault and choose **More** > **Change Retention Rule for Existing Backups**.
